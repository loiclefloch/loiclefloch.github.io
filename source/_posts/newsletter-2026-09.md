## Building with AI

After harness engineering we are now in the beginning of the IA Factory era. Take a look at [why Software Factories Fail](https://github.com/humanlayer/advanced-context-engineering-for-coding-agents/blob/main/wsff.md) (or in [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=htM02KMNZnk&t=27219s)).
A forceful critique of lights-off coding factories, arguing that harnesses cannot replace accountability and system understanding : safe agentic coding still needs product review, architecture, program design, and vertical slices.
